Learning Together

How AI Reshapes Interaction in Multilingual Families

Application submitted · Decision pending
LEGO Foundation Fellowship · Proposed research

Status as of October 26, 2026.

How does generative AI enter a multilingual family's everyday learning, and whose knowledge becomes recognized? Learning Together centers the languages, experiences, and ways of learning that children and caregivers already bring to shared activity.

The Research Focus

The proposed study examines how children and caregivers evaluate AI-generated responses and negotiate participation and authority. It attends to decisions to use, question, refuse, or defer AI, treating those decisions as part of family interaction.

The Proposed Approach

Longitudinal interactional ethnography will follow recurring activities such as shared reading, homework, storytelling, and family inquiry. Close analysis of interaction over time will examine how families work with multilingual resources and make sense of AI together.

Why Everyday Interaction Matters

What an AI system produces is only part of the encounter. How family members interpret a response, bring other knowledge to bear, and decide what to do next matters for shared inquiry. The project aims to inform resources that support multilingual participation and productive struggle in family learning.

Connections Across My Research

This direction extends my research on multilingual families' meaning-making in museums into family life with generative AI. It retains a focus on talk, gesture, shared attention, and the material and textual resources through which people learn together.
