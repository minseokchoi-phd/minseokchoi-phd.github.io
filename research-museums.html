Multilingual Family Literacy in Science Museums

This line of research examines how multilingual families make meaning together during visits to science museums. Drawing on video-based interaction analysis, I investigate how talk, gesture, gaze, movement, and exhibit texts are coordinated in the moment.

Research Questions

  • How do multilingual families collaboratively make sense of scientific ideas?
  • How do children and caregivers mobilize their full communicative repertoires?
  • How do museums support or constrain multilingual participation?

Selected Publications

  • Choi (2024). Translanguaging through Pointing Gestures.
  • Choi (2022). A Sojourning Multilingual Family's Sense-Making in a Science Museum.

Current Directions

My current work extends this research into public libraries and other community learning environments, with particular attention to professional vision and institutional recognition of multilingual family competence.
