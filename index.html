About

My research sits at the intersection of early literacies, multilingual family learning, and the study of libraries, museums, and other public institutions as everyday learning environments.

Drawing on ethnographic, discourse analytic, and multimodal interaction analysis, I look closely at how people use language, gesture, objects, and stories to participate in learning. I ask what counts as literacy, who gets to be seen as literate, and how institutions can learn to notice and support multilingual family competence.

Research

  • Multilingual family life, learning, and meaning-making
  • Early literacies across homes, libraries, and museums
  • Translanguaging and multimodal interaction analysis
  • Professional noticing and institutional seeing
  • Ethnography of everyday learning interaction
  • Public learning design and equitable access
