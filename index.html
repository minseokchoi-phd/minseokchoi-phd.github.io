About

My research sits at the intersection of early literacies, interactional sociolinguistics, multilingual family learning, and teacher education.

Drawing on ethnographic, discourse analytic, and multimodal approaches, I examine how people use language, gesture, objects, stories, and relationships to participate in learning across everyday settings.

Across my work, I seek to understand and support more equitable educational futures by recognizing the knowledge, resources, and experiences that children, families, and communities bring to learning.

Research

  • Interaction, language, and meaning-making
  • Multilingual family life and learning
  • Early literacies across everyday settings
  • Translanguaging and multimodal interaction
  • Teacher noticing and professional vision
  • Equity, social justice, and humanizing approaches to education
